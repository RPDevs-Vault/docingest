06 / WHY THE NAME

Pink Sheets was the over-the-counter quotation system run by the National Quotation Bureau from 1913, printed on pink paper. It listed what the major exchanges would not carry, and trades happened by negotiation rather than through a central book.

That is exactly this desk, a century later.

=====================================

No order book to read, no curve to fight. You state what you want to move; the people who want it compete for it in the dark; one transaction closes the whole thing.

PARAMETERS, IN THE OPEN.
------------------------

PARAMETERVALUEWHY

MINIMUM SIZE

$25,000 notional

Below this a pool is genuinely cheaper. The desk should not compete with itself.

AUCTION WINDOW

2 – 15 minutes

Long enough to price, short enough to carry little risk.

Long enough for a human maker to price it, short enough to carry little market risk.

BID COVERAGE

Checked on-chain at bid time

A signed order, checked against the chain before it counts.

BID COLLATERAL

Bids arrive as hashes and are opened only once the window closes. No maker reads another maker’s price early, and neither does the desk.

One atomic transaction

Both legs move together or the state never changes. The contract never holds either asset.

FEE

None

The desk charges nothing. The seller already pays in spread.

The desk charges nothing. The seller already pays in spread; charging them twice kills the flow we want.

PRIVACY

Intent public, bids sealed

Competition needs the intent public. The bids must not be.

The intent has to be public for competition to exist. The bids must not be.

FAILURE MODES

WHAT CAN GO WRONG.
------------------

### NOBODY BIDS

The window closes empty. The intent expires and you pay nothing, but everyone now knows you wanted to move size.

→

Intents can be posted to a maker subset instead of the whole room.

### THE WINNER DOES NOT PAY

A bid is only worth what backs it. Without backing, the winner is whoever never intended to settle.

→

Coverage is re-checked before settlement; if it fails, the fill passes to the second-best bid and the maker takes a strike.

### THE MARKET MOVES MID-WINDOW

Fifteen minutes is long enough for the reference price to run away from your intent.

→

You set a reserve. Below it nothing settles and the intent simply expires.

BOUNDARIES

### NOT AN ORDER BOOK

No resting liquidity, no queue, nothing to place, cancel or camp on. An intent exists for minutes and then it is gone.

### NOT AN AMM

No curve, no LPs, no impermanent loss. The desk holds no inventory and takes no side of the trade.

### NOT AN RFQ CHAT

Quotes are signed orders, checked against the chain and settled by contract. Nobody is trusting a screenshot in a group chat.
